Delete your account

VENDÚ · Last updated: 6 October 2026

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open VENDÚ and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your account (email, name, profile picture and user ID).
  • Your usage counter on our server.
  • Everything the app stored on your phone: drafts, copied photos, templates, replies, preferences, profile name and photo.

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Photos and text sent to the AI are not stored on our servers, so there is nothing else for us to delete.

If you no longer have the app or cannot sign in

Email us from the Google account address you used at appialabs.vendu@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my VENDÚ account". We will delete your account and data as soon as possible and at most within 30 days.

What may remain outside our control

Google and Cloudflare, as service providers, may keep technical logs for a limited time under their own policies. If you granted VENDÚ access to your Google account you can also revoke it at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
